5. Data Retention

  • Local data (decks, progress): stays on your device until you delete the App or clear its data.
  • Cloud data (signed-in users): retained while your account exists. You can request deletion — see Section 7.
  • Firebase Analytics: events are retained for the period set in our Firebase project (Google allows up to 14 months).
  • Shared decks and leaderboard entries: stay public until the owner removes them or we remove them on request. Deleting your account in the App does not remove them automatically.
  • Cached read-aloud audio at Cloudflare and AI requests at Google: held according to those providers' own policies; we do not control their retention.
  • Crash reports: retained for 90 days.

6. Children's Privacy

Fliply is not directed at children under the age of 13. The App asks you to confirm that you are 13 or older; Firebase Analytics and the crash reporter start only after that answer. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are under 13, please do not sign in and do not enter personal information. If we learn that we have collected personal data from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly. Contact us at apexforge.games@gmail.com if you have concerns.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — request a copy of data we hold about you
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion (Right to Erasure) — request that we delete your account and associated data. You can delete your account in the App (Settings → Delete account), which removes your synced decks, settings and profile record and your sign-in account. Shared decks and leaderboard entries are not removed by this; to have them removed, email apexforge.games@gmail.com with subject "Delete my account". We will complete the deletion within 30 days.
  • Portability — export your decks at any time via Settings → Data → Export backup
  • Withdraw consent — switch off "AI deck creation" and "Online voices" at any time in Settings. After that, nothing is sent for AI or server read-aloud, and read-aloud uses your phone's own voice.
  • Advertising ID — the App does not use your device advertising ID, shows no ads and has no tracking prompt. The advertising-ID permissions and ad-related Analytics settings are switched off in the App.

For GDPR (EU) or Israeli Privacy Law (חוק הגנת הפרטיות) requests, contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including HTTPS for all data in transit and Firebase Security Rules for cloud-stored data. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.
